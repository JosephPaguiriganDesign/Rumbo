Lisbon to Málaga, two weeks, one ball.

Rumbo is a small summer trip for players aged 12 to 17. We train twice a day with our own coaches, play friendlies against local clubs, and eat an unreasonable number of pastéis de nata in between.

01

Two coasts, seven days each

We land in Lisbon, spend a week on the coast west of it, then take a short flight south to Andalusia for the second week. The line below fills in as you scroll. It's the only part of the trip that goes exactly to plan.

Week one: the Lisbon coast

We base ourselves in a guesthouse-style hotel on the coast west of Lisbon, close enough to the city for a day out and close enough to the sea that nobody can claim they didn't get a swim. Sessions run on rented grass and 3G pitches nearby, with our own coaches.

02

How you get on the plane

Five steps. The first one is free and takes about a minute.

  1. 1

    Put your name down

    Fill in the interest list below. It's not an application and it's not a commitment.

  2. 2

    Have a chat

    A 20-minute video call with a parent and the player. We'll ask about position and experience, and you can ask us anything, including the awkward stuff.

  3. 3

    Lock in a spot

    A $500 deposit holds a place in one of the two age groups. The balance is due on 1 April 2027.

  4. 4

    Fly out together

    Most of the group flies into Lisbon on Sunday 11 July. We'll help families with flight timing so nobody lands alone.

  5. 5

    Train, play, repeat

    Two weeks of sessions and friendlies. Every player gets a short written note on what they did well and what to work on next.

03

A fortnight, roughly

This is a sample. The real itinerary gets confirmed with families in spring 2027, once pitches and opponents are locked. Fixtures depend on other people's calendars, so treat match days as "hoped for" until we say otherwise.

  1. 01

    Sun 11 Jul Land in Lisbon

    Airport pickup, check in, a slow walk to the water. Early night.

  2. 02

    Mon 12 Jul First session

    Team meeting after breakfast, then a light session so the coaches can see who's who.

  3. 03

    Tue 13 Jul Two-a-day

    Morning technical work, evening small-sided games. Video review over dinner.

  4. 04

    Wed 14 Jul Friendly No. 1 match day

    Against a local club, if the calendar cooperates. Everyone plays.

  5. 05

    Thu 15 Jul Lisbon, on foot

    Morning session, then Belém and the old town. Tram rides are optional; ice cream is not.

  6. 06

    Fri 16 Jul Our own mini-tournament

    Groups mixed across ages where sensible, coaches refereeing badly on purpose.

  7. 07

    Sat 17 Jul Friendly No. 2 match day

    Then a beach afternoon at Estoril to recover.

  8. 08

    Sun 18 Jul Fly Lisbon to Málaga

    A short flight, then check in and a walk along the port.

  9. 09

    Mon 19 Jul Settle in

    Session in the evening when it's cooler. Heat is a real thing here.

  10. 10

    Tue 20 Jul Two-a-day

    Early start, long lunch, late session.

  11. 11

    Wed 21 Jul Friendly No. 3 match day

    Against a local side, kick-off time set by whoever owns the pitch.

  12. 12

    Thu 22 Jul The Alcazaba

    A morning walk through the fortress, a light session after.

  13. 13

    Fri 23 Jul Friendly No. 4 match day

    The last game. Whole group, both age bands, families welcome to watch if they're in town.

  14. 14

    Sat 24 Jul Debrief and goodbyes

    Written notes for each player, a long lunch, then airport runs.

Who's looking after your kid

The short version: enough adults, all checked, all known to you by name before anyone boards a plane.

Team sheet · sample staff

  • 1MarcoHead coach, sessions and selection for friendlies
  • 2AnaGoalkeeper coach, also runs video review
  • 3DiegoAssistant coach for the older group
  • 4SofiaWelfare lead and chaperone, first-aid certified
  • 5JosephRuns Rumbo. Logistics, parents, and bags.

Names above are sample. Final staff and full names go to families before registration closes.

1:6adults to players
At least one adult for every six players, all day, including evenings and travel days. Players room in groups of three or four, same age band and same gender, never alone with a single adult.
Staff checks
Every adult travelling with the group completes a criminal background check before registration closes. Parents can ask to see confirmation for any of them.
Medical and insurance
Group travel medical insurance is included in the price. We collect a medical form and emergency contacts before departure, and one staff member holds current first-aid certification.
Parent updates
A short message and a photo or two every evening, and a phone number that gets answered. If something's wrong, you hear it from us first.
